Req 4. Order management

Logged-in users add books, change quantities, and remove items before submitting. Once submitted, the order moves to history and is read-only.

ii.

Req 7. CSRF protection

Every form on this page (update, remove, submit) ships with a CSRF token. Posts without the token are rejected before they touch the database.

iii.

Req 8. Server-side recalculation

On submit, the server recalculates the total from the database using the prices captured when items were added. Any tampering with prices in the browser is ignored.
