How this page meets the brief.

Click any underlined feature above to jump to its explanation.

i.

Req 1. Single-book detail

A dedicated page per book showing title, author, price, description, and cover. Open to anyone.

ii.

Req 7. CSRF protection

The Add to basket form carries a CSRF token. Django middleware rejects any submission missing the token, so a different site cannot post here on the user's behalf.

iii.

Req 9. XSS prevention

All fields are auto-escaped at render time. Even an admin who pasted a script tag into a title would see it as plain text in the browser, never executed.

iv.

Innovation. Admin-managed stock

Customers see only the availability state, never the count. Staff edit the integer through the Django admin, and the order pipeline decrements stock atomically when an order is submitted — so a sold-out title cannot be over-ordered even by two shoppers checking out at once.
