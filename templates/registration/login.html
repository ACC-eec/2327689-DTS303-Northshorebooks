Req 3. Authentication

Authenticates the username and password against Django's hashed password store. The browser never sees the stored hash, only a session cookie on success.

ii.

Req 7. CSRF protection

The login form carries a CSRF token. A login attempt forged by another site is rejected by middleware before the password is ever checked.

iii.

Req 8. Brute-force protection

After 5 failed attempts in 5 minutes the username is locked out. The session ID is also rotated on every successful login so an old session cookie cannot be reused.
